I am Yanyan Cheng. Mind-Note is a personal site: it records special-needs and mental-health projects on one side, and study notes, book reviews, and self-reflection on the other.

This is not a public psychoeducation portal, and not a clinic website. It is closer to a lab notebook that keeps being rewritten — the structure should stay clear, and the content should be able to grow.

## What I care about

- Special needs: how learning difference is translated among classroom, family, and assessment reports.
- Mental health: turning clinical language into something the public can use, without thinning complexity into slogans.
- Neuroscience and traditional psychology: walking between circuits and personality theory instead of picking a side.

## How to use this site

- **Projects**: programmes and resources I am running or curating.
- **Study notes**: five sessions — Educational, Clinical, Mental Health (public), Neuroscience, Psychology.
- **Book reviews**: organised by genre.
- **Reflection**: short listed pieces that turn theory back on myself.

The site is meant to keep changing. New pages and edits go through a password-protected admin.

## Principles

- No diagnosis, no prescriptions, and no reproduction of DSM-5 criteria text.
